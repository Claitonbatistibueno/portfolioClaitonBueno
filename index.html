about

From the ledger to the codebase

I spent more than ten years in Brazil working in back-office operations, finance and accounting processes, and payroll. Much of that time was spent with the systems themselves: financial systems, HCM platforms, identity and access management, and customising business processes for different requirements.

That work made me curious about how those systems are built. I moved to Ireland, completed a QQI Level 5 in Software Development, and joined the Immersive Software Engineering programme at the University of Limerick in September 2026.

I like to understand why something exists before I build it. The parts of software I enjoy most so far are DevOps and cloud: pipelines, deployments, and making sure a team's work fits together.
