A problem I want to work on: VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA)

ViDA is the EU's reform of the VAT system. It moves cross-border business-to-business trade to structured e-invoicing and near real-time digital reporting to tax authorities, with a standard electronic invoice format mandatory from July 2030. Companies in Ireland and across the EU will need finance and ERP systems that can issue and report invoices this way.

This is familiar ground for me. Latin America made the same move years ago, and I have worked on finance and payroll system projects in countries such as Brazil, Chile and Mexico, where electronic invoicing and digital statutory reporting are already mandatory.

  • E-invoicing
  • Digital reporting requirements (DRR)
  • Real-time tax reporting
  • Continuous transaction controls
  • VAT One Stop Shop (OSS)
  • NF-e and SPED (Brazil)
  • eSocial (Brazil)
  • DTE (Chile)
  • CFDI (Mexico)

About

From the ledger to the codebase

I hold a degree in Accounting and Finance and have worked in the field for more than ten years, always close to payroll and accounting operations. My work was never limited to one country: I delivered international payroll and finance projects for operations in Brazil, Italy and across Latin America.

As an ERP functional consultant I sat between the client and the developers: mapping processes, configuring the system and writing the specifications for what had to be built. That work made me curious about how the systems themselves are engineered.

After moving to Ireland I completed a QQI Level 5 in Software Development, and in September 2026 I joined the Immersive Software Engineering programme at the University of Limerick. So far, the part I enjoy most is DevOps: pipelines, deployments and making a team's work fit together.
